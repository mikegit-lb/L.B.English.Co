Before you begin

Good questions.
Make a confident choice.

Which exam gives me the right advantage?

Start with the requirement, not the name. IELTS and TOEFL assess English proficiency; SAT supports college admission; YDT, YDS and YÖKDİL serve different Türkiye requirements.

Is SAT an English test or a college-readiness test?

No. The full SAT includes Math and Reading & Writing. This studio’s SAT coaching covers Reading & Writing only.

Are these official questions or scores?

No. All starters are original practice. Feedback describes the activity you completed and does not predict an official score.

Can I get a plan made for me?

Yes. Choose private 1:1, a VIP group of 3–5 or exam consultancy, then prepare a brief with your goal and timeline.
