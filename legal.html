The studio

L.B. English Co. is the learning studio name shown on this site. Verified legal operator details and a customer contact channel have not yet been supplied. The site therefore does not accept bookings or payments.

Your information stays with your practice

Lesson completion, study plans, saved resources, checklist progress and resource notes are stored in your browser. They are not sent to a tutor or server. The coaching brief is generated on your device as a downloadable text file; downloading it does not send an enquiry. Avoid adding sensitive personal information to notes or shared downloads.

Browser storage, clearly explained

The site uses localStorage for learning preferences and progress. It does not load analytics, advertising trackers, external fonts or embedded social media. Your hosting provider may process ordinary request logs when the site is hosted. Browser site-data controls can remove your saved progress. If storage is blocked or full, you can still practise, but new progress may not survive a reload.

Coaching and booking

Private 1:1 teaching, VIP groups of 3–5 and exam consultancy are described as service options. Lesson dates, availability, prices, cancellation conditions and a verified booking channel must be provided before any booking can be confirmed. A downloaded coaching brief is only a document you can keep or share.

Resources and downloads

The nine resources and the mini-lessons on this site are free practice tools. A resource can be edited in your browser, downloaded as a text file, or printed using your browser’s Print / Save as PDF function. There is no paid checkout, premium delivery system or refund transaction on this site.

Independent practice and realistic expectations

The mini-lessons are original practice content. L.B. English Co. is not affiliated with or endorsed by IELTS, College Board or the other exam owners named here. The language practice check cannot establish an official level, score or equivalency. The IELTS calculator only averages and rounds entered scores. Coaching outcomes depend on the learner and are not guaranteed. SAT coaching on this site covers Reading & Writing, not Math.

Keyboard, screen and motion preferences

Navigation and learning controls use semantic links, buttons, form labels and native dialogs. Dialogs support Escape and keyboard focus containment. The layout adapts to smaller screens and honours reduced-motion preferences. Resource text can be downloaded for use in other reading tools.

Official exam references

Check current exam details directly with the exam owner.
